Scene 1: Tamandua

The sun rises over the Osa Peninsula, casting a golden glow through the lush canopy of the rainforest. The air is thick with humidity, rich with the scent of damp soil and blooming orchids. Birdsong echoes from the treetops, mingling with the steady buzz of insects.


You drive into Tamandua, a remote field station operated by KWF and your base for the Rainforest DeepData Expedition. As your team finishes unloading the gear, a sense of anticipation fills the air. Beyond the clearing, the dense jungle beckons-alive with mystery, challenges, and discovery. How will you begin your expedition from here?
