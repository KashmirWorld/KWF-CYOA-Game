Scene 4: Rainforest Night Survey

As night falls over the rainforest, your team sets up camp. The forest comes alive in new ways—the hum of insects crescendos, frogs call from hidden pools, and the distant hoots and howls of nocturnal species echo through the trees.

You huddle over a laptop by red flashlight, reviewing the day’s footage and preparing for a rare chance to capture nocturnal data. The AI system needs night recordings to better recognize threats in low-light conditions.
