How to Play

First, listen to each species call and study its role in the rainforest. Then the game will begin the challenge phase. During the challenge, drag the mystery sound token onto the correct species card. You need 3 correct matches to continue.

Howler Monkey

Booming canopy calls, often used as a warning signal and indicator of forest disturbance.

Great Tinamou

Ground-based repetitive calls that can reveal shifts in understory habitat conditions.

Scarlet Macaw

Loud canopy calls that help indicate forest health and nesting activity.
