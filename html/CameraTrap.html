Set up a Camera Trap

You mount the camera trap on a sturdy tree facing the game trail. As you adjust the settings, the jungle quiets—only the distant call of a howler monkey breaks the stillness. You also deploy an acoustic sensor nearby, calibrated to capture ultrasonic calls and low-frequency movements.

Soon, the forest will record its own secrets. You mark the location on your GPS and prepare to move on.
