Deploy Sensors

You trek deeper into the rainforest, moving carefully through the dense understory. The jungle teems with life-distant bird calls, the rustle of small mammals, the hum of insects. As you reach a shaded clearing near a slow moving stream, you recognize an oppotunity: this is a prime spot to begin data collection. Your gear includes a mix of tools-camera traps, acoustic sensors, a portable environmental lab, and simulation software back at the field station. From here, you can take your expedition in several directions, depending on your priorities and remaining resources.
